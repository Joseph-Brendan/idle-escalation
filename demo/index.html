Idle Escalation

Click a field and wait. The focus ring starts as a soft tone and deepens every two seconds. At full color it starts to thicken, half a pixel at a time. Type anything and it goes back to calm.

Who it helps

People who freeze or stareThe ring grows until it catches the eye, pulling attention back to the field so the user can start typing.
People who look awayAfter a phone check, another tab or another window, the ring marks the exact spot. No scrolling. No searching.

How it behaves

  • Soft startStep 1 already meets 3:1 contrast. Focus is always visible.
  • Color, then widthThe tone deepens first. Once it is full, the outline thickens by 0.5 px per step.
  • Holds at the peakNo loops, blinking or pulsing. It stops at 4 px and waits.
  • Calm on activityAny keystroke sends the ring back to step 1 at 2 px.
  • Nothing movesThe ring is an outline, so the field and the page never shift.
  • Respects settingsNo easing under reduced motion. System colors in forced-color mode.
